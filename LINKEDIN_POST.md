## 1. Main LinkedIn Post Copy

Most AI feedback tools suffer from a fatal flaw: complete amnesia. Every time you ask a question, the LLM starts from zero—forgetting past complaints, investigations, and whether a bug was already fixed last month.

We built FeedbackMind AI to solve this by pairing Groq Cloud with Hindsight, an episodic memory engine. Instead of re-reading raw logs, the AI retains customer feedback as a cognitive memory graph, recalls longitudinal context, and connects multi-month feedback across releases.

Result: AI that tracks what customers report, what was fixed, and what to prioritize next.
