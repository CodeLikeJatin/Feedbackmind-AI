## Selected Strongest Title

### **Primary Selection:**
> ### **Building FeedbackMind AI: How Hindsight Gives LLMs Long-Term Memory**
> *(9 words)*

### Rationale:
1. **Clear Product Identity:** Leads directly with `FeedbackMind AI`, establishing the system being discussed.
2. **Immediate Problem & Solution Pairing:** Explicitly frames what is missing in standard LLMs (*they lack long-term memory*) and how it was solved (*How Hindsight Gives LLMs Long-Term Memory*).
3. **Natural Vendor Attribution:** Highlights `Hindsight` organically without sounding like promotional filler.
4. **Professional & Technical Tone:** Avoids exaggerated clickbait ("You Won't Believe...") while maintaining high technical intrigue for engineers, product managers, and AI researchers.
5. **Length Compliance:** At 9 words, it is concise, memorable, and well under the 10-word constraint.
